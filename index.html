How the CSL Plasma referral works

CSL Plasma's current referral program requires the referred person to be a new donor with no prior donation history at CSL Plasma. The referral code and first donation must be within seven days of each other, and the new donor must complete two successful donations within 30 days of the first donation to qualify for referral rewards. Requirements and rewards can change, so check CSL Plasma's current terms before donating.

Important steps for a new donor

  1. Use the referral link above.
  2. Follow CSL Plasma's current new-donor registration instructions.
  3. After receiving a Donor Number, activate iGive Rewards in the CSL Plasma app if required.
  4. Apply the referral code according to CSL Plasma's current instructions and timing requirements.
  5. Complete the required donations and maintain an active iGive Rewards account if applicable.

Referral rewards are subject to CSL Plasma's eligibility requirements and current referral-program terms. The referral program is currently described by CSL Plasma as valid for eligible donors in the United States and void in Puerto Rico.
